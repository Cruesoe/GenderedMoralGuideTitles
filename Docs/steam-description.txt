[b]Moral Guide roles get male and female titles, the same way Leader already does.[/b]

Made by request.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]New ideoligions generate a female form of the title when the noun has one, such as priest and priestess or abbot and abbess.
[*]Assigned pawns show the title that matches their gender.
[*]The ideoligion editor has separate male and female name fields for Moral Guide roles.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Requires Ideology.
Safe to add to an existing save.
